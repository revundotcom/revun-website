# Competitor teardown — what they do that we don't

Scraped 2026-08-06 with Firecrawl. Five sites, all supplied by the client:
`ringcentral.com`, `eliseai.com/platform-overview`, `doorloop.com`,
`brickwiseai.com`, `centralcoms.com/landing`.

Everything below is quoted from the live pages, not paraphrased from memory.

---

## The finding that matters

**Every competitor leads with numbers. We have none.**

| Site | Proof numbers on the page |
|---|---|
| CentralComs | `3x` `5x` `40 hrs` `52.6 hrs` `45 min` `2 min` `22%` `1.04M` `120k` `42K` `5 days` |
| RingCentral | `60%` `$1.7M` `30%` `600k` `1000+` `200+` |
| DoorLoop | `281%` `29%` `20%` |
| EliseAI | `201%` `203%` |
| Brickwise | `3B` |
| **Revun** | **none** |

CentralComs puts them in the H1 itself:

> **"3x more doors. Higher revenue per unit. Same staff."**

Ours is a capability statement:

> "Run your entire property business on one system"

Both are fine sentences. Only one of them is a claim a buyer can check.

EliseAI and CentralComs each run a whole section for this — *"Proof in the
Results"*, *"Results from teams using CentralComs."* We have no equivalent
anywhere on the site.

**This is the single biggest gap, and it is the one thing we cannot close
ourselves.** The numbers have to come from the client. Inventing them would be
the first thing a prospect checks and the fastest way to lose the account.

---

## What we need from the client

Four numbers unlock a proof section that matches every competitor:

1. **Response time** — before vs after Revun. *(RingCentral claims 60%)*
2. **Hours saved per property manager per week.** *(CentralComs claims 40 hrs)*
3. **Volume** — messages, calls, or work orders handled to date. *(RingCentral: 600k businesses)*
4. **Doors or units under management on the platform.** *(CentralComs: 3x more doors)*

Even one real figure is enough to build the band. Zero means we stay the only
name on the list with nothing to show.

---

## Second gap: the AI has no name

Every competitor personifies it. We describe it.

| Site | How they name it |
|---|---|
| Brickwise | **"Meet Alice: Your AI Property Manager"** — a name, a role, a personality |
| RingCentral | **AIR** (Receptionist), **AVA** (Virtual Assistant), **ACE** (Conversation Expert) |
| EliseAI | "Agentic AI that powers every stage of the resident journey" |
| DoorLoop | "AI operational intelligence — built in" |
| **Revun** | "AI & Automation" — a feature label |

Brickwise's whole homepage is built around Alice. "Meet Alice" and "How she
works" are H2s. That is a brand decision, not a copy tweak, so it needs the
client's call — but it is why their page feels like a product and ours feels
like a spec sheet.

---

## Third gap: social proof scale

- EliseAI — *"Trusted by Over 700 of the Top Property Management Companies"*
- DoorLoop — *"Loved by tens of thousands of customers"*
- RingCentral — *"Loved by over 600,000 businesses"*, plus *"Gartner Magic
  Quadrant Leader for the 11th year in a row"*, plus named logos (Golden State
  Warriors, San Francisco Symphony)
- **Revun** — "Built with owners, PMCs, and brokerages". No count, no names.

## What we already fixed from this research

The client's note was: *"follow these guidelines whenever you are presenting or
showcasing our communications."* Applied to `/features/communications/` —
headings moved from capability to outcome, facts unchanged:

| Was | Now |
|---|---|
| Unified Messaging | Every thread stays with the unit |
| Built-in Voice Calls | Call without exposing personal numbers |
| Video Conferencing | Meet face to face, on the record |
| Auto-Transcription | Never lose what was said on a call |
| Secure and Compliant | Data stays in the country it belongs to |
| Saved Recordings | Proof of every conversation, kept |

The homepage AI bento already matches the competitor pattern of showing the AI
doing work rather than describing it.

---

## What we can honestly claim today

Real, already-published, verifiable:

- `4.7 / 5` operator satisfaction
- `13` Canadian provinces and territories
- `50` US states + DC
- `40+` integrations

These are **coverage** claims. Coverage answers "can you serve me?" — it does
not answer "will this make me money?", which is the question every competitor
on this list answers with a number in the first screen.
